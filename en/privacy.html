What data we process

If you contact us through the enquiry form, we process the data you enter in it:

  • your name and surname,
  • your phone number,
  • your e-mail address,
  • the location of the site (town or village),
  • a description of your project and the type of service.

We don't ask for anything else and we don't collect anything you haven't told us yourself.

Why we process it

We use the data only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and arrange a site visit. If you become our customer, we continue to use it to perform the contract and keep our accounts, as required by law.

The legal basis is your consent (for an enquiry you send), performance of a contract (for work carried out) and compliance with legal obligations (for accounting records).

What we don't do with it

We don't use it to send advertising, we don't pass it to any third party for marketing purposes and we don't sell it.

Who may see it

Only our employees who handle enquiries have access to the data. We also use the following services to process it:

  • Web3Forms — handles sending the form to our e-mail. The data passes through their server.
  • Our web and e-mail hosting provider — this is where received messages are stored.

If we add analytics or other tools in future, we will list them here and ask for your consent to cookies.

How long we keep it

  • Enquiries that don't lead to work: a maximum of 12 months from the last contact, then we delete them.
  • Completed jobs: for the duration of the contract and then for the warranty period and statutory periods (accounting records usually 10 years).

Your rights

In relation to your data you have the right:

  • to know what data we hold about you (right of access),
  • to have it corrected if it is inaccurate,
  • to have it erased if we no longer need it,
  • to restrict its processing,
  • to object to the processing,
  • to withdraw your consent at any time,
  • to lodge a complaint with the Czech Data Protection Authority (uoou.cz).

Just write to info@mostavty.cz — we will reply within one month at the latest. We don't charge anything for handling a request.

Cookies

This website does not store any tracking or advertising cookies in your browser. We don't use analytics tools or embedded elements from other sites that would set cookies.

Changes

If the way we process data changes, we will update this text and state the date of the last change.
