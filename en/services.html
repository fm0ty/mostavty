Our services

Our main work is complete renovations — we take over a flat or a house and hand it back finished. If you need just one thing, we can do that too.

Our speciality

Complete renovations

One budget, one deadline, one person you deal with. No coordinating five separate tradesmen.

Flat renovation

From demolishing the bathroom core to the last skirting board. New water, waste and electrical installations, bathroom and WC, floors, plaster, doors, painting. We also handle approval from the owners' association.

  • Usually 6–10 weeks
  • From approx. 12,000 CZK/m² (approx. 480 EUR)
  • Free survey and quote
Service detail

House renovation

Family houses, cottages, terraced and older properties. Façade insulation, a new roof, replacement windows, layout changes, plumbing. We can deal with damp walls and work on load-bearing structures.

  • Usually 3–6 months
  • Drawings and structural engineer included
  • In stages to suit your budget
Service detail

Our most common single job

Bathrooms and prefab bathroom cores

We demolish the old laminate bathroom core from a prefab flat and build a brick or plasterboard bathroom with new installations in its place. We handle waterproofing, waste pipes, electrics and ventilation.

A finished bathroom usually takes 14–21 days. You'll keep a working toilet throughout if the layout allows — we'll tell you up front.

What's included

  • Demolition of the core and rubble removal
  • New water and waste pipes
  • Electrical work including bonding
  • Waterproof tanking and sealing tapes
  • Wall and floor tiling, grouting
  • Fitting sanitary ware and taps
  • Made-to-measure shower or bath
  • Final clean

Individual items

Individual jobs

You don't need a full renovation. We'll come for a single room, a single wall or a single floor.

Plastering and renders

Skim plastering walls and ceilings, levelling walls, smoothing out unevenness, renovating and lime renders, skim coats ready for paint or tiles.

Service detail

Plasterboard

Partitions, suspended ceilings, boxing in services, loft conversions, acoustic and fire-rated structures. Including jointing and sanding to a paint-ready finish.

Service detail

Flooring

Self-levelling screeds, vinyl, laminate, wooden floors, tiles, carpets. Including subfloor preparation, skirting boards and threshold profiles.

Service detail

Painting and wallpapering

Priming, painting, coatings, wallpapering and stripping old wallpaper. We cover your furniture and clean up after ourselves.

Demolition and bricklaying

Removing partitions, new openings and lintels, bricking up, chasing out channels for services, rubble removal by skip.

Water, waste, electrics

New water and waste pipes, relocating outlets, electrical installation including the consumer unit and certification. Our own plumber and electrician.

  • Concrete and anhydrite screeds
  • Wall and floor tiling
  • Staircases
  • Terraces and paving
  • Damp proofing
  • Loft conversions and dormers
  • Minor roofing work
  • Waste removal and disposal

Can't find what you need? Give us a call — if we don't do it ourselves, we usually know someone who does.

What we don't do

So we don't waste your time: we don't build houses from the ground up on greenfield sites, we don't supply fitted kitchens and we don't do garden design. We focus on renovations — that's what we're good at.
