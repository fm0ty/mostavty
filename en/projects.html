What a renovation looks like in practice

An example from a specific job — so you know what to expect from working with us.

3-bed flat, Jihlava — prefab building, 68 m²

The owners bought the flat in its original 1980s condition. They wanted to open the kitchen into the living room, replace all the services and get rid of the laminate bathroom core.

What we did

  • Demolition of the bathroom core and the non-load-bearing wall between the kitchen and the living room
  • New water, waste and electrical installations including the consumer unit
  • A brick bathroom with a walk-in shower and a separate WC
  • Suspended plasterboard ceilings with recessed lighting
  • Skim plaster on all walls, vinyl flooring throughout
  • New internal doors and lined frames

The result

Finished in 7 weeks, budget met with no extra work. The owners moved in a week after handover.
