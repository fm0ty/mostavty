How it works

Five steps from your call to a finished job

No surprises along the way. You know in advance what will happen, when and at what cost.

You call us

You describe what you need. We'll tell you straight away whether it's a job for us and roughly what price range to expect.

We come and survey

The site visit is free and without obligation. We measure up, take photos, check the condition of the services and point out any hidden risks.

You get a quote

An itemised quote within 48 hours — materials and labour listed separately, so you can see what you're paying for. The price in the contract is fixed.

We build

We work to a schedule you have in writing. One site manager is your contact throughout. We send progress photos.

Handover and clean-up

Handover protocol, cleaning, waste removal. You get the warranty in writing and we respond to any complaint within three working days.

What you get on top

A fixed price

The quote is signed into the contract. Extra work only goes ahead with your written approval — no unexpected bills at the end.

Deadlines we keep

You get the schedule at the start. If a delay is our fault, the contract includes a penalty — payable by us, not you.

One contractor

Clean-up included

Where we work

Vysočina and Prague

Our home region is Vysočina — Jihlava, Třebíč, Havlíčkův Brod, Žďár nad Sázavou, Pelhřimov and the surrounding area. This is where we're quickest and travel is included in the price.

We also take on work in Prague, most often complete flat renovations. For larger jobs outside Vysočina we always tell you up front how it affects the price and the timeline.
