Who we are

A construction company from the Vysočina Region. We carry out renovations with our own people — which is why we stand behind the result.

Our story

From a team of bricklayers to a renovation company

We started out as a small team taking on bricklaying work around Jihlava. Over time more and more customers told us they didn't want to find an electrician, a plumber and a floor fitter separately — they wanted one firm to handle the lot.

So we gradually built up the team. Today MOstavTY has its own bricklayers, electrician, plumber, tiler and floor fitter. For specialist work — structural engineering, drawings, certification — we have long-standing partners we've worked with for years.

We mainly do complete renovations of flats and houses. But when a customer calls because they only need one bedroom plastered, we don't send them elsewhere.

What we stand by

  • We tell you the truth up front. When something is wrong — damp walls, tricky structural work, an unrealistic deadline — we say so before signing, not halfway through the job.
  • The price holds. What's in the quote is what you pay. Extra work only with your written approval.
  • We clean up after ourselves. Continuously, not just at the end. Rubble removal is included in the price.
  • One contact. You have the name and phone number of the site manager who knows about everything.

Where we work

Vysočina is home, Prague is a trip out

We're at home in Vysočina — Jihlava, Třebíč, Havlíčkův Brod, Žďár nad Sázavou, Pelhřimov. Here we can often come out for a survey the same week and we don't charge for travel.

In Prague we mainly take on complete flat renovations, where it makes sense to have a team on site throughout. For individual jobs in Prague we look first at whether it makes sense for you at all — and we'll tell you straight.

Paperwork in order

  • Liability insurance up to 10,000,000 CZK — if we damage something while working, the insurer deals with it, not you.
  • Trade licence for carrying out, altering and removing building works.
  • Certified electricians for electrical installations — the certificate is part of the handover.
  • A contract for work on every job, including smaller ones. Including the quote, the schedule and the warranty.
  • Proper invoices and full bookkeeping. We're not a firm that builds on a handshake over coffee.

We're happy to show you any of these documents — just ask during the site visit.
