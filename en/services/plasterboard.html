Plasterboard

Partitions, suspended ceilings, boxing in services and complete loft conversions. We include jointing and sanding, so the wall is ready to paint straight away.

Request a free quote Call us

When plasterboard helps

Plasterboard is quick, light and clean. It suits you when you want to divide a space without loading the floor slab, hide services, straighten an uneven wall or lower a ceiling and conceal lighting and ventilation in it.

What matters is that the structure is built properly. The wrong stud spacing, missing acoustic insulation or unfinished joints show up as cracks and as hearing every word from the next room.

What we'll build for you

  • Dividing partitions — single or double boarded, with insulation
  • Suspended ceilings — flat, coffered or with lighting recesses
  • Wall linings — to conceal services and straighten walls
  • Loft conversions — sloping ceilings, knee walls, vapour barrier
  • Acoustic structures — for flats, bedrooms and home offices
  • Fire-rated structures — to the required fire resistance
  • Structures for wet areas — moisture-resistant board for bathrooms
  • Access panels and penetrations — for reaching the services

How we do it

We use system profiles and boards from a single manufacturer, because that's the only way the certified performance figures apply — particularly for acoustics and fire resistance. We fill partitions with mineral wool; without it a partition is just a drum.

We fill and sand joints to a Q2 to Q3 standard depending on what follows — Q2 is enough under ordinary paint, while under raking light or a dark colour we recommend Q3 so the joints don't show. We agree this in advance, because it affects the price.

What to expect from us

  • Floors and furniture covered, dust extraction while sanding
  • Flatness checked with a straight edge, not by eye
  • We clean up after ourselves and take the offcuts away
  • We'll do a single room, not just a whole flat

Quick facts

  • Duration: a partition usually 1–3 days
  • Price: depends on the type of structure
  • Jointing and sanding included
  • Acoustic and fire-rated systems
  • Suitable for wet areas (bathrooms)
  • We'll come for a single room
