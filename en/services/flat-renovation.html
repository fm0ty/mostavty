Hand us the keys and we'll give you back a finished flat. Demolition, installations, bathroom, floors, plaster and painting — all in one quote and one timeline.

What a flat renovation involves

The process is similar for prefab and older brick flats. First the flat is cleared and everything that can go is stripped out — the laminate bathroom core, non-load-bearing partitions, old floors and tiles. Then new services are run, because boxing forty-year-old pipes behind new tiles makes no sense.

A large part of the work is only visible while it's happening: water and waste pipes, electrical installation, waterproofing in the bathroom. This is exactly where the difference shows between work that lasts and work that leaks in five years.

What the job includes

  • Survey, measuring up and an itemised quote
  • Demolition including rubble removal and disposal
  • New water, waste and electrical installations including the consumer unit
  • Electrical installation certificate
  • Bathroom and WC — brick or plasterboard, waterproofing, tiling, sanitary ware
  • Plasterboard partitions and suspended ceilings
  • Skim plastering and levelling walls, or skim coats
  • Flooring — screed, vinyl, laminate, wood or tiles
  • Internal doors and lined frames
  • Painting throughout
  • Ongoing and final cleaning
  • Handover protocol and warranty certificate

What affects the price

The quote varies mainly according to the condition of the flat and the materials you choose. The biggest factors are:

  • Work on load-bearing structures — requires a structural engineer and a notification
  • The extent of the services — replacement only, or relocating outlets too
  • The bathroom — a basic bath versus a walk-in shower and designer sanitary ware
  • Materials — there's a big difference between laminate and a wooden floor
  • Access to the site — a lift versus the fourth floor without one

That's why we don't quote blind over the phone. We come, take a look and write a quote that holds.

What about living there during the work

We don't recommend living in the flat during a complete renovation — there are water and power cuts and a lot of dust. Most customers stay elsewhere for that time. If that isn't possible, we plan the work in stages, but it extends the timeline.

Quick facts

  • Duration: usually 6–10 weeks
  • Price: from approx. 12,000 CZK/m² (approx. 480 EUR)
  • Warranty: 60 months on the work
  • Quote: free within 48 h
  • We arrange owners' association approval and notifications
  • Rubble removal and cleaning included

How the renovation runs

Survey and quote

We measure the flat, check the services and send an itemised quote within 48 hours.

Contract and schedule

We sign a contract for work with a fixed price. You get a week-by-week schedule.

Demolition and removal

We strip out the bathroom core, partitions and old surfaces. Rubble goes out by skip.

Services and first fix

New installations, bricklaying, waterproofing, plasterboard. Then an inspection before everything is covered up.

Surfaces and finishing

Renders, skim plaster, tiling, floors, doors, sanitary ware, painting.

Handover

Cleaning, handover protocol, warranty certificate and certification. The flat is ready to move into.
