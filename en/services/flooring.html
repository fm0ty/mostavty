Flooring

Self-levelling screeds, vinyl, laminate, wooden floors and tiles. What we watch most is the subfloor — no floor covering lasts on an uneven one.

Request a free quote Call us

The subfloor decides everything

Not even the most expensive covering lasts on an uneven or damp floor. Vinyl copies every bump, laminate starts to creak, tiles crack along the grout lines. That's why we always measure the subfloor — flatness with a straight edge and, on concrete, the residual moisture as well.

If the subfloor is poor, we tell you in advance and include the cost of the screed. It's an item customers often don't see in quotes from other firms, and then it comes as an unpleasant surprise.

What flooring we lay

  • Vinyl flooring — glued or click, water resistant, suitable for kitchens and bathrooms
  • Laminate — the quickest and most affordable option for rooms
  • Wooden floors — three-layer and solid, glued or floating
  • Tiles and large formats — including grouting and silicone in the corners
  • Carpets — broadloom and carpet tiles
  • Self-levelling screeds — for levelling the subfloor
  • Anhydrite and concrete screeds — new floor build-ups
  • Skirting boards and profiles — wood, MDF, aluminium

How we proceed

  1. We remove the old covering and skirting and clean the subfloor.
  2. We measure the flatness and, on concrete, the moisture.
  3. Where needed we prime and apply a levelling screed.
  4. We let it dry — usually 1–3 days for a screed, depending on thickness.
  5. We lay the underlay and the covering with expansion gaps at the walls.
  6. We fit the skirting and threshold profiles.
  7. We clean up and take the waste away.

Underfloor heating

Not every covering or underlay is suitable for underfloor heating. We choose a material with the right thermal resistance and follow the heating commissioning protocol — otherwise the floor can deform or the joints can come apart. If you have underfloor heating, tell us at the survey.

Quick facts

  • Duration: a room usually 1–2 days
  • Price: depends on material and subfloor
  • Flatness and moisture of the subfloor measured
  • Screed and priming as required
  • Beading and threshold profiles included in the labour
  • Suitable for underfloor heating
